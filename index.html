About

John is a lyric tenor, with a degree in vocal performance from Arizona State University. He has received instruction from prominent artists such as Dr. Amanda DeMaris, Dr. Justin Carpenter and Mary-Sue Hyatt. In 2021, John was recognized by the National Association of Teachers of Singing by receiving first place in the Arizona Division Vocal Competition and second place in the Cal-Western Region. He as also made semi-finals for N.A.T.S. 2022 national vocal competition.
